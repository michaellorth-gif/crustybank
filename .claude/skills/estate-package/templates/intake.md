# Estate Package Intake

## PART A — The gate (any ✗ → route out with explanation; flags → Mike)

- [ ] Estate comfortably below federal estate-tax exposure (verify current
      exemption; when in doubt, ballpark gross estate including life insurance)
- [ ] No special-needs beneficiary, Medicaid planning, or asset-protection ask
- [ ] No disinheritance of spouse/child; no unequal split the family doesn't expect
- [ ] No capacity or undue-influence concerns (client engaged directly; beneficiary
      not steering) — ANY doubt goes to Mike before drafting
- [ ] No closely held business, foreign assets, significant minerals, or
      out-of-state real property
- FLAG (doesn't route out): blended family; minor children; prior will exists

## PART B — Family & fiduciaries

- Client: full name, DOB, county of residence, marital status
- Spouse: full name (and whether doing a mirror-image package — bundle pricing)
- Children: name, DOB (adult/minor), from this marriage or prior
- Deceased children leaving descendants?
- **Executor** + first and second alternates (name, relationship, city)
- **Guardian for minor children** + alternate
- **Financial agent (POA)** + alternate; effective immediately or on incapacity?
- **Medical agent (MPOA)** + alternate
- Anyone the client wants explicitly EXCLUDED from any role?

## PART C — Distribution plan

- Residuary plan: [all to spouse, then children equally per stirpes] or [other — describe]
- Specific gifts (keep short; each adds contest surface): item → person
- Contingent trust age for young beneficiaries (default 25)
- Ultimate contingent beneficiaries (if the whole family fails): [names / charity]

## PART D — Assets (overview, not an inventory)

- Homestead (county); other real property (route out if out-of-state)
- Approximate ranges: bank/brokerage, retirement, life insurance face value
- Beneficiary-designation audit list (retirement/insurance pass OUTSIDE the will —
  client must confirm designations match the plan; document that advice)

## PART E — Logistics

- Existing will/POA to revoke? (collect copies)
- Signing ceremony: two disinterested witnesses arranged (NOT beneficiaries), notary
- Engagement letter (package scope, flat fee, what's excluded) signed; fee paid
