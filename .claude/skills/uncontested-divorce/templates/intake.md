# Uncontested Divorce Intake — Tier 1 Gate + Drafting Facts

## PART A — The gate (all boxes must clear; any ✗ → route out with explanation)

- [ ] One spouse: Texas domiciliary ≥ 6 months AND county resident ≥ 90 days (which
      spouse, which county, since when)
- [ ] No children of the marriage under 18 (or still in high school/disabled —
      escalate); none expected — wife not pregnant (ask directly)
- [ ] No real property owned by either spouse (if owned: Tier 2 quote — deed +
      deed of trust to secure assumption)
- [ ] No retirement account DIVISION requested (mutual waivers OK; QDRO = Tier 2)
- [ ] Full agreement on every asset and every debt (test it: "who keeps the tax
      refund?" "whose name is the Visa in and who pays it?")
- [ ] No family violence history or fear of the other spouse (screen alone, not in
      the spouse's presence; any hit → Mike immediately)
- [ ] Respondent's location known; respondent expected to sign the waiver
- [ ] Neither spouse active-duty military deployed (SCRA screen)
- [ ] Conflict check BOTH spouses — no prior adverse representation

## PART B — Drafting facts

### Parties
- Petitioner (our client): full name, DOB, last 3 of DL & SSN (decree ID
  requirements), address, phone, email
- Respondent: full name, address for waiver mailing, email/phone, last-known
  employer (fallback service)
- Date and place of marriage; date of separation
- Name change: does either party want a former name restored? Exact spelling.

### Property (community and separate — list everything, even "nothing")
- Vehicles: year/make/model/VIN, titled to, lien, who keeps
- Bank/credit union accounts: institution, last 4, who keeps
- Retirement: each party's accounts (institution, approx balance) — confirm
  mutual waiver
- Personal property: any single item over ~$500 in dispute? (furniture/electronics
  usually "in possession of" language)
- Life insurance policies; tax refunds/liabilities (current + prior years);
  businesses, crypto, storage units, animals
- Separate-property claims either party asserts

### Debts
- Each credit card / loan: creditor, last 4, approx balance, whose name, who pays
- Any joint debt staying joint? (discourage; flag to Mike)

### Logistics
- Preferred filing county (must satisfy 90-day rule)
- Client goals on timing; any date constraints (insurance open enrollment,
  remarriage plans — remind of 31-day rule)
- Engagement letter signed (petitioner-only + Tier 1 scope + conversion triggers);
  stage-1 fee paid; filing fee quoted at county's current rate
