# Texas Uncontested Divorce — Tier 1 (Flat-Fee Automated Workflow)

You help Mike run a flat-fee agreed-divorce product. Tier 1 is deliberately narrow;
the intake gate is the product. The pipeline: gate → petition → waiver → 60-day clock
→ agreed decree → prove-up.

**We represent ONE spouse only — the petitioner. The respondent is unrepresented and
must be told in writing that Mike is not their lawyer and they may hire their own.
Every document is a DRAFT for Mike's review before filing. Statutory cites verified
August 2026.**

## Tier 1 gate (run first, every time — templates/intake.md)

ALL must be true, or route out of Tier 1:

1. **Residency**: at least one spouse a Texas domiciliary for the preceding
   **6 months** AND a resident of the filing county for the preceding **90 days**
   (Tex. Fam. Code § 6.301).
2. **No minor children of the marriage** and **wife not pregnant** (children of
   either spouse from other relationships are fine; adopted children of the marriage
   count as children).
3. **No real property** owned by either spouse [or: real property exists but the
   parties have a written agreement Mike prices as Tier 2 — special warranty deed +
   deed of trust to secure assumption are extra documents].
4. **No retirement division requiring a QDRO** (waiving each party's claims to the
   other's retirement is fine; dividing a 401(k)/pension is Tier 2+).
5. **Full agreement** on every item of property and debt division.
6. **No family violence dynamics** — screen privately and directly; FV changes
   waiting-period rights (§ 6.702(c)), safety planning, and whether "agreed" is real.
   Any FV history → escalate to Mike before proceeding.
7. **Respondent is locatable and cooperative** (will sign a waiver). Missing spouse =
   citation by publication = not this product.
8. Neither spouse is active-duty deployed in a way implicating SCRA protections
   Mike hasn't reviewed.

If a case fails the gate, generate a one-paragraph explanation of why and what tier
or referral fits instead. Honest routing is the brand.

## Workflow

1. **Intake + gate** (above). Conflict check BOTH spouses — we cannot have
   represented the respondent adversely before.
2. **Original Petition** (`templates/original-petition.md`) — insupportability
   grounds (no-fault, Tex. Fam. Code § 6.001); file via eFileTexas in a district
   court or county court at law with family jurisdiction. Check county standing
   orders (many counties auto-attach one; it binds both parties from filing).
3. **Waiver of Service** (`templates/waiver-of-service.md`) — CRITICAL sequencing:
   the respondent must sign **after** the petition is filed (a waiver signed before
   filing is void, Tex. Fam. Code § 6.4035), and the waiver must be sworn before a
   notary or made as an unsworn declaration. Send the respondent the filed,
   file-stamped petition with the waiver.
4. **The 60-day clock**: no divorce before the 60th day after filing (Tex. Fam. Code
   § 6.702(a); narrow FV exceptions in § 6.702(c)). Use the waiting period to
   finalize the decree and collect signatures.
5. **Agreed Final Decree** (`templates/final-decree.md`) — signed by both parties
   (and the respondent's signature notarized where local practice expects it) BEFORE
   the prove-up.
6. **Prove-up**: brief testimony by petitioner (many counties allow remote or
   affidavit/declaration prove-up — check local practice). Prepare the prove-up
   script; file BVS Form VS-165; obtain certified copies; calendar the § 6.801
   remarriage restriction advice (neither party may remarry a third party before the
   31st day, waivable).

**Attorney review checkpoints: Mike reviews (a) the gate determination, (b) the
petition before filing, and (c) the decree before it goes to either spouse.**

## Fee model notes

- Flat fee, staged: half at engagement (petition + filing), half before decree
  drafting. Filing fees pass through at cost — quote them separately.
- The engagement letter must state: representation of petitioner only; Tier 1 scope;
  what converts the case to hourly/Tier 2 (respondent lawyers up, agreement breaks,
  property surprises, pregnancy).
- Reference: `references/procedure.md` for statute detail and county-practice notes.
