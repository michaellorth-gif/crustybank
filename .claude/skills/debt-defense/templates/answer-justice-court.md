## DEFENDANT'S ANSWER

Defendant [CLIENT_NAME] files this Answer to Plaintiff's Petition and states:

**1. Appearance.** Defendant appears and answers in this debt claim case governed by
Rule 508 of the Texas Rules of Civil Procedure.

**2. General Denial.** Defendant generally denies each and every allegation in
Plaintiff's Petition and demands that Plaintiff be required to prove its claims as
required by law. Tex. R. Civ. P. 502.6, 92.

**3. [IF SWORN PETITION] Verified Denial of Account.** Defendant denies that the
account which is the foundation of Plaintiff's action is just or true, in whole or in
part, and denies each and every item of the account. Tex. R. Civ. P. 93(10), 185.

**4. [IF ASSIGNEE PLAINTIFF] Verified Denials — Standing, Capacity, and Assignment.**
Defendant denies the genuineness of each alleged assignment, transfer, or endorsement
by which Plaintiff claims to own the alleged account, and denies that Plaintiff has
standing or legal capacity to sue or is entitled to recover in the capacity in which
it sues. Tex. R. Civ. P. 93(1), 93(2), 93(8).

**5. Affirmative Defenses.** Pleading further, Defendant asserts:
   - [IF TIME-BARRED] The claims are barred by the statute of limitations. Tex. Civ.
     Prac. & Rem. Code § 16.004.
   - [IF PAYMENT FACTS] Payment and accord and satisfaction.
   - [IF ID-THEFT FACTS] Defendant is not the person who incurred the alleged debt;
     any obligation arises from mistaken identity or identity theft.
   - [OTHER — ONLY WITH FACTS: release, waiver, estoppel, usury]

**6. Attorney's Fees.** Defendant denies that Plaintiff is entitled to recover
attorney's fees, and denies that all conditions precedent to any fee recovery,
including presentment, have been satisfied.

**7. Prayer.** Defendant prays that Plaintiff take nothing, that Defendant recover
costs, and for all other relief to which Defendant is entitled.
