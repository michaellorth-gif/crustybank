## COMPANY AGREEMENT OF CLEARFEE LEGAL PLLC
### A Texas Professional Limited Liability Company

This Company Agreement (the "Agreement") of ClearFee Legal PLLC (the "Company")
is made effective as of [EFFECTIVE DATE — on or after the SOS filing date] by
Michael L Orth, as the sole Member.

### Article 1 — Formation and Purpose

**1.1 Formation.** The Company was formed as a Texas professional limited
liability company by the filing of a Certificate of Formation with the Texas
Secretary of State on [FILING DATE], File No. [SOS FILE NUMBER], under Title 3
and Title 7 of the Texas Business Organizations Code (the "Code").

**1.2 Name.** The Company's name is "ClearFee Legal PLLC." The Company may
conduct business under that name and any assumed name the Member adopts in
compliance with law and the Texas Disciplinary Rules of Professional Conduct.

**1.3 Purpose.** The Company's sole purpose is to engage in the practice of law
in the State of Texas and activities necessary or incidental to that
professional service, as permitted for a professional limited liability company
under Chapter 301 of the Code. The Company shall not engage in any other
business.

**1.4 Professional Requirements.** Every Member, manager, and officer of the
Company who renders professional services on its behalf shall be an attorney
licensed and in good standing with the State Bar of Texas. No person who is not
so licensed may hold a membership interest, and no membership interest may be
transferred to any such person. The Company shall not permit any nonlawyer to
direct or regulate the professional judgment of a lawyer rendering services on
its behalf, consistent with Rule 5.04 of the Texas Disciplinary Rules of
Professional Conduct.

**1.5 Principal Office; Registered Agent.** The principal office is at
[PRINCIPAL OFFICE ADDRESS], [CITY], Texas. The registered agent and registered
office are as stated in the Certificate of Formation, as amended from time to
time.

**1.6 Term.** The Company continues until dissolved under Article 8.

### Article 2 — Member and Membership Interest

**2.1 Sole Member.** Michael L Orth is the sole Member and holds 100% of the
membership interest.

**2.2 Additional Members.** Additional Members may be admitted only (a) with the
written consent of the Member, (b) if licensed as required by Section 1.4, and
(c) upon executing a joinder to this Agreement. Admission of an additional
Member shall be accompanied by an amendment of this Agreement addressing
capital, allocations, management, and buy-out terms.

**2.3 Limited Liability.** The Member is not personally liable for the debts,
obligations, or liabilities of the Company solely by reason of being a Member.
Nothing in this Agreement limits the personal professional liability of an
attorney for that attorney's own professional acts, errors, or omissions, which
remains as provided by law.

### Article 3 — Capital

**3.1 Initial Contribution.** The Member has contributed [INITIAL CAPITAL —
e.g., $[AMOUNT] cash and/or the assets on Schedule A] to the Company.

**3.2 Additional Contributions.** The Member is not required to make additional
contributions, but may do so at any time.

**3.3 No Interest; No Withdrawal.** No interest accrues on contributions, and
the Member may not withdraw capital except as distributions under Article 5.

### Article 4 — Management

**4.1 Member-Managed.** The Company is managed by its Member. The Member has
full authority to manage the Company's affairs, bind the Company, open and
control bank and trust accounts, engage employees and contractors, and take any
action permitted by law.

**4.2 Officers.** The Member may appoint officers with such titles and
authority as the Member designates in writing.

**4.3 Trust Accounts.** All client funds shall be held in one or more IOLTA or
other trust accounts maintained in the Company's name in compliance with Rule
1.15 of the Texas Disciplinary Rules of Professional Conduct and the rules of
the Texas Access to Justice Foundation. Trust-account signatory authority is
limited to licensed attorneys of the Company. Flat fees are handled according
to the Company's written engagement agreements and applicable ethics rules
regarding earned and unearned fees.

**4.4 Standards.** The Member shall cause the Company to maintain professional
liability insurance in an amount the Member deems prudent, to keep books and
records as required by Section 6.1, and to comply with all rules governing the
practice of law.

### Article 5 — Allocations and Distributions

**5.1 Allocations.** All profits, losses, and tax items are allocated to the
sole Member.

**5.2 Distributions.** The Member may cause the Company to distribute available
cash at such times and in such amounts as the Member determines, provided that
no distribution may be made if, after giving it effect, the Company's
liabilities would exceed the fair value of its assets or the Company would be
unable to pay its debts as they come due (Code § 101.206). Client trust funds
are never Company property and are never available for distribution.

### Article 6 — Books, Records, Accounting, and Tax

**6.1 Records.** The Company shall keep at its principal office the records
required by Code § 3.151 and § 101.501, including this Agreement, the
Certificate of Formation, tax returns for the last six years, and financial
statements.

**6.2 Fiscal Year; Method.** The fiscal year is the calendar year. Books are
kept on the [cash / accrual] method as the Member's CPA advises.

**6.3 Tax Classification.** So long as the Company has a single Member, it is
disregarded as an entity separate from its Member for federal income tax
purposes unless the Member elects otherwise. [CPA TO CONFIRM — the Member may
elect S-corporation treatment via Form 2553 if advantageous.] The Company shall
file Texas franchise tax reports and Public Information Reports annually.

### Article 7 — Transfers; Death or Disqualification of the Member

**7.1 Restrictions.** No membership interest may be transferred, pledged, or
encumbered except to a person eligible under Section 1.4, and any purported
transfer to an ineligible person is void.

**7.2 Death, Incapacity, or Disqualification.** If the Member dies, becomes
incapacitated, or ceases to be licensed to practice law in Texas, the
membership interest may be held by the Member's estate or legal representative
only for the purpose and period the Code permits for winding up or transferring
the interest to an eligible attorney (Code §§ 301.007–301.009), and the
Company shall take the steps required by the State Bar for the protection of
clients, including the designation in Section 7.3.

**7.3 Successor Attorney Designation.** The Member designates [SUCCESSOR
ATTORNEY NAME, Bar No. ___] — a licensed Texas attorney — as the attorney
authorized, upon the Member's death or incapacity, to review client files,
notify clients, protect deadlines, and wind up or transfer client matters and
trust funds in accordance with the Texas Disciplinary Rules. [Mike: this is the
single most important line in the document for a solo practice — name someone
and confirm they accept.]

### Article 8 — Dissolution and Winding Up

**8.1 Events.** The Company dissolves upon (a) the Member's written decision,
(b) the events in Section 7.2 if no eligible successor holds the interest within
the period the Code permits, or (c) as otherwise required by law.

**8.2 Winding Up.** On dissolution the Member (or successor attorney) shall wind
up the Company: complete or transfer client matters and return client property
and trust funds first; pay or provide for creditors; then distribute remaining
assets to the Member. A Certificate of Termination shall be filed with the
Secretary of State.

### Article 9 — Indemnification

To the fullest extent permitted by the Code, the Company shall indemnify the
Member and officers against claims arising from their good-faith service to the
Company, excluding liability for the person's own professional negligence,
willful misconduct, or knowing violation of law. The Company may purchase
insurance for this purpose.

### Article 10 — General Provisions

**10.1 Governing Law.** Texas law governs this Agreement.

**10.2 Entire Agreement; Amendment.** This is the entire company agreement. It
may be amended only by a writing signed by the Member.

**10.3 Severability.** Invalid provisions are severed; the rest remains in
force.

**10.4 Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds the Member and the Member's
successors and permitted assigns.
