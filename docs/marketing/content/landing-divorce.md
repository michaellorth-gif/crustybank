## THIS FLAT FEE IS FOR YOU IF…

- You and your spouse **agree on everything** — property, debts, all of it
- **No children** of the marriage under 18 (and no baby on the way)
- **No house or land** to divide, and no retirement accounts to split
- Your spouse will **sign the paperwork** voluntarily

Not all of those true? You may still have a very manageable case — it just
isn't this flat-fee product, and we'll tell you honestly what track fits
and what it costs before you commit to anything.

## WHAT TO EXPECT

1. **Online intake** — a few minutes; our screening tells you immediately
   whether the flat-fee track fits.
2. **We prepare and file** the Original Petition in your county.
3. **Your spouse signs a waiver** — no process server showing up at their job.
4. **The 60-day waiting period** — Texas law requires it in nearly every case;
   we use the time to finalize your agreed decree.
5. **One short court appearance** (some counties allow remote), and it's done.

Realistic timeline for an agreed case: **about 61–90 days**, driven mostly by
the mandatory waiting period.

## IMPORTANT — WE REPRESENT ONE SPOUSE

A lawyer can't represent both sides of a divorce, even a friendly one. We
represent **you**; your spouse is free to have their own attorney review
everything before signing.

## FAQ

**Can I get my former name back?** Yes — we include the name restoration in
the decree at no extra charge. Just ask.

**What does the flat fee not cover?** Contested issues. If your spouse hires a
lawyer to fight, stops cooperating, or the agreement falls apart, the case
converts to our standard rates — we tell you in writing before anything
changes.

**What about the filing fee?** Court filing fees are set by each county and
are paid separately at cost — typically a few hundred dollars. We quote your
county's exact number up front.
