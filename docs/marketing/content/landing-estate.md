## WHAT'S IN THE PACKAGE

| Document | What it does |
|----------|--------------|
| **Simple will** | Says who inherits, names your executor, protects young beneficiaries with built-in trust terms, and is "self-proved" so probate is smoother |
| **Statutory durable power of attorney** | Lets someone you trust handle finances if you can't |
| **Medical power of attorney** | Names who makes medical decisions if you can't speak for yourself |
| **Directive to physicians** | Your end-of-life treatment wishes, in writing, so your family never has to guess |

Everything is signed in **one appointment** at our office with the witnesses
and notary handled for you — Texas signing rules are strict, and a
badly-executed will can fail completely.

## FAQ

**Do I need to update it later?** Revisit after marriage, divorce, a birth, a
death, or a major asset change — or about every three years. We'll remind you.

**What about my 401(k) and life insurance?** Those pass by beneficiary
designation, outside your will — part of our process is a checklist to make
sure those designations match your plan.

**Married — do we each need our own?** Yes, each spouse signs their own set;
the couples bundle prices the pair together.
