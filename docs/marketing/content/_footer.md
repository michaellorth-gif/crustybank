engagement agreement. Results depend on the facts of each case, and no result
is guaranteed. [IF FEE MENTIONED: The quoted flat fee covers the services

MENTIONED: If there is no recovery, you owe no attorney's fee; you may remain
responsible for case expenses and amounts owed to medical providers or lien
holders as stated in the written fee agreement.]
