## HOW THE REDUCED FEE WORKS

- **[25]% of the recovery** if your case resolves before suit is filed
- If a lawsuit becomes necessary, the fee converts to our standard rate —
  stated in your written agreement from day one, never sprung on you later
- **No recovery, no attorney's fee.** Case expenses and amounts owed to your
  medical providers or insurers are your responsibility and come out of the
  recovery, exactly as your written agreement spells out

## WHICH CASES QUALIFY

The reduced fee is for wrecks where fault is clear — you were rear-ended, the
other driver was cited, or the other driver was intoxicated — with injuries
treated by doctors and an insured defendant. **Not every case qualifies**, and
that's the honest part of the deal: disputed-fault, catastrophic-injury,
commercial-truck, and wrongful-death cases need — and get — the full
traditional approach at our standard fee. Our intake tells you which track
fits before you sign anything.

## WHAT WE DO ON EVERY QUALIFYING CASE

Notify the insurers and shut down calls to you · protect the evidence ·
make sure your own policy's benefits (PIP/MedPay) aren't left unclaimed ·
gather every record and bill · prepare a complete demand · negotiate ·
and resolve the medical liens so the case is actually *finished*.

## DON'T WAIT

Texas generally gives you **two years** from the wreck to file suit — but
evidence, witnesses, and camera footage disappear in weeks, not years. And
never give a recorded statement to the other driver's insurance company
before getting advice.

## FAQ

**Is the reduced fee lower-quality representation?** No — it's the same
attorney and the same standards. The discount comes from process efficiency
on cases that fit the streamlined track, not from doing less.

**What if my case doesn't qualify?** Then it needs the traditional approach,
and we'll either handle it at our standard contingency or tell you plainly
what we recommend. Either way you'll know within a day of your intake.

**What does "no fee if no recovery" really mean?** You owe no *attorney's
fee*. Case expenses and your medical bills/liens are separate, and the written
agreement — which you get before hiring us — spells out exactly how those
work. We'll walk you through it line by line.
