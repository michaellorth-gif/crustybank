# Recall campaign — DREO DR-HSH015 Space Heater (CPSC 27-054)

> STATUS: DRAFT — not approved, not filed. Landing page: `/recall/dreo-space-heater` (preview with `?preview=draft`)
> Decision memo: About 338,800 heaters sold in the U.S. through Costco, Best Buy, Lowe's, Home Depot, Kohl's and QVC from September 2025 through October 2026, with 17 reported fires, three with property damage and one hand burn; going into winter, a few of those fires will be in Texas. The importer, Hesung Innovation Corp. doing business as DREO, is a New Jersey company with a real brand, and every retailer in the chain is a collectible domestic big-box store, so this is not the uncollectible-storefront problem. The case that matters is a post-recall house fire with a burn injury, or a sale made after DREO already had incident reports; Texas protects a nonmanufacturing retailer absent a Chapter 82 exception, so the manufacturer and importer are the primary targets. The strongest defense is the low incident rate (17 in 338,800), the free-replacement remedy, and assumption of risk by a consumer who kept using the heater after the October 29 notice. Verdict: ADVERTISE, search ads only to start; the national firms will have pages up within days, so the window is now.
