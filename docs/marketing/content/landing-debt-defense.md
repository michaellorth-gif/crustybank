## FAQ

**What if I actually owe the money?**
You still have rights: the amount, the ownership of the debt, and the
paperwork all matter, and Texas exemption law affects what a judgment could
even reach. Many cases resolve for far less than the amount sued for. No
outcome is guaranteed — but doing nothing guarantees the worst one.

**What if I ignore it?**
The collector can get a default judgment, which can lead to frozen bank
accounts and years of interest. Judgments in Texas can last a decade and be
renewed.
