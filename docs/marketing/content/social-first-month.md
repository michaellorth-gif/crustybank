## Week 1 — Debt defense

**Post 1 [EDUCATIONAL]**
Myth: "If I ignore the debt collection lawsuit, maybe it'll go away."
Reality: ignoring it is how the collector wins automatically. In Texas justice
court you generally have 14 days after being served to respond. The companies
filing these suits count on silence — don't give it to them.

**Post 2 [EDUCATIONAL]**
Something most Texans don't know: for ordinary consumer debts (credit cards,
medical bills), Texas generally does NOT allow wage garnishment. The threats
you're imagining may not match what the law actually permits. Know your
position before you negotiate against yourself.

## Week 2 — Expunction

**Post 4 [EDUCATIONAL]**
"The charge was dismissed, so my record is clean." Unfortunately, no — in
Texas the ARREST stays on your record until a court orders it expunged. If a
background check keeps surfacing a case that went nowhere, that's fixable for
many people.

**Post 5 [EDUCATIONAL]**
Expunction vs. sealing, in one breath: expunction destroys the record of a
qualifying arrest (dismissals, acquittals, no-charge arrests); sealing hides a
completed deferred-adjudication case from public view. Which one fits depends
entirely on how your case ended.

## Week 3 — Estate package

**Post 7 [EDUCATIONAL]**
If you die without a will in Texas, state law decides who inherits — a
formula, not your wishes. With stepchildren, second marriages, or an unmarried
partner, the formula's answer is often exactly wrong. An hour of paperwork
prevents years of mess.

**Post 8 [EDUCATIONAL]**
The most important estate documents work while you're ALIVE: a power of
attorney (someone can handle finances if you can't) and a medical power of
attorney (someone can talk to your doctors). Without them, your family may
need a court order just to help you.

## Week 4 — Divorce + MVA

**Post 10 [EDUCATIONAL]**
Agreed divorce in Texas, honest version: there's a mandatory 60-day waiting
period after filing, one spouse files and the other signs a waiver, and if you
agree on everything, most cases finish in about 61–90 days. It does not have
to consume a year or a fortune.

**Post 11 [EDUCATIONAL]**
After a car wreck: (1) photos of everything, (2) see a doctor promptly — gaps
in treatment hurt both your health and your claim, (3) do NOT give the other
driver's insurance a recorded statement before getting advice. Their adjuster's
job is to minimize what they pay you.
